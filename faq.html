Frequently Asked Questions

What is Nova Bridge X (NVBX)?

NVBX is a utility token on BNB Smart Chain focused on affordable international transfers and future payment solutions.

Why was NVBX created?

To provide a practical blockchain-based option for small international transfers and digital payments.

Which network does NVBX use?

BNB Smart Chain (BEP-20).

How can I buy NVBX?

You can buy NVBX through PancakeSwap using BNB.

Can I send NVBX internationally?

Yes. NVBX can be transferred directly between wallets worldwide.

What are the transaction costs?

Transaction costs depend on BNB Smart Chain network conditions and are typically low.

Does NVBX guarantee profits?

No. NVBX is a utility token. Cryptocurrency markets involve risk.
